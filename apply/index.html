EIRcamp in under 30 seconds

There's no school or degree for becoming an entrepreneur in residence. Most people learn the job by doing it.

EIRcamp gives you 12 weeks of doing it: building a real new business, with founders who hire EIRs as your mentors.

Go to the application See the 12-week plan

The 12-week plan

Four stages, three weeks each, following the life of a new business: spot it, build it, run it, then land your next role or raise for your own.
