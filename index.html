Who it's for

You don't need a co-founder, a registered company or a technical background. You need a problem you care about and six to eight hours a week.

You have a job and an idea

You want to find out whether the idea is worth leaving for, without quitting first. Sessions are on Saturday mornings and weekday evenings.

You're a student or just graduated

You'd rather build something than wait for placements, and you want structure and people who'll tell you the truth about your idea.

You've started but you're stuck

You have a product or a prototype but no paying customers yet, and you're not sure what to do next.

Probably not a fit if you're already past ₹10 lakh in revenue or raising a Series A. The program is built for the stage before your first real customers.

How a week works

Every week has one topic. You learn it at your own pace, practise it live with your cohort, and hand in something real by Sunday.

What you'll have by week 12

Not a certificate. The work itself.

  • 20 customer interviews, written up, with the patterns you found
  • A working first version of your product, live at a link you can share
  • Your first 10 users, or ten conversations that told you to change direction
  • A price and the numbers behind it: what a customer costs to win and what they're worth
  • A plan for setting up the company, or the registration already done
  • A pitch deck and a three-minute pitch, delivered at demo day to mentors and angel investors

Questions

Do I need a startup idea to apply?

You need a problem you want to work on. It's fine if you don't have a solution yet: weeks 1 to 3 are about picking and testing the problem.

Can I do this while working full-time?

Yes. Most of the work is self-paced, and the live sessions are on Saturday morning and Wednesday evening. Plan for six to eight hours a week.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted the same day. You need to hand in at least 10 of the 12 weekly assignments to finish the program.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. In weeks 4 to 6 you'll build your first version with no-code tools, or run it by hand. Technical founders can build it however they like.

Will you invest in my startup or help me raise money?

We don't invest, and we can't promise funding. Fellows who are ready pitch at demo day to a panel of mentors and angel investors, and in week 10 you'll build a plan for grants, incubators and investors.
