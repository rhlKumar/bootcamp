Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data EIRcamp collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Gumbo Tech Pvt. Ltd., which runs EIRcamp, collects and uses personal data when you visit eircamp.com, apply to a program or take part in one. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other laws that apply.

2. What we collect

  • From your application: your name, city, email, phone number, LinkedIn profile, current work, years of experience, goals and answers to our questions. We save your contact details when you finish the first step of the form, even if you don't send the rest.
  • If you join a program: payment confirmation, attendance, the work you hand in, mentor feedback, and recordings of live sessions you take part in.
  • When you visit the site: basic technical data such as your IP address, browser and pages visited, which our hosting provider logs automatically.

We don't collect or store your card or bank details. If you pay online, the payment provider handles them.

3. Why we use it

  • To review your application, contact you about it and set up a call, including if you started the form but didn't finish it.
  • To run the program: sessions, mentor matching, feedback, recordings and demo day.
  • To send you updates about EIRcamp. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To improve our programs and keep the site working and secure.
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your mentor and cohort: mentors see your application and the work you hand in. Other participants see what you share in sessions and your peer group.
  • Service providers who work for us: Google (application forms and spreadsheets), GitHub (website hosting), Meta (ad measurement), video-call and payment providers, and WhatsApp or email for messages. They use your data only to provide their service.
  • Authorities: government or regulatory bodies, when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal data. Some of these providers may store data outside India.

5. Cookies

We use the Meta Pixel to measure how well our ads on Facebook and Instagram work. It sets cookies and sends Meta information such as the pages you visit, your browser and IP address, and the fact that you sent an application. It doesn't send the answers in your application. Meta uses this under its own privacy policy, and you can control ad tracking in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings or with your browser's tracking protection. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

6. How long we keep it

We keep application data for up to 12 months after the cohort you applied for ends, then delete it unless you've joined a program. For participants, we keep program records for as long as we need them to run the program and the alumni community, and longer only where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it, or withdraw your consent to our using it,
  • name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.

Email us from the address you used with us, with your full name. We'll reply within 30 days. If you withdraw consent while you're in a program, we may not be able to keep providing it to you.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data and limit who can see it. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that data will never be lost or accessed without permission. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Our programs are for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the date at the top of this page when we do.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal data, email support@gumbotech.in. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
