What's included

Everything below is covered by the one fee. There are no add-ons.

  • 12 Saturday workshops

    90 minutes, live, with exercises in small groups. Recorded.

  • 12 office hours

    Wednesday evenings. Bring a question or a draft.

  • Self-paced lessons

    Two to three hours a week of short videos, case studies and templates.

  • 20 hours a week of project work

    You build a real new line of business across the 12 weeks, and your mentor reviews it every week.

  • Your own mentor

    The same mentor for all 12 weeks, with written feedback on every assignment.

  • A peer group

    Five people aiming for similar roles, who meet weekly and review each other's work.

  • Demo day and mock interviews

    Pitch to a panel in week 12, and practise EIR and founder's office interviews.

Week by week

Open any week to see the live sessions, the self-paced material and what you hand in. Together, the hand-ins make up your EIR portfolio.

Who should apply

EIRcamp is designed for people with 1 to 7 years of work experience and some exposure to startups. You don't need to have founded a company.

Professionals with 1–7 years' experience

You're in consulting, product, strategy, sales or operations, and you want to move into roles where you build new things.

Founder's office and strategy teams

You already work close to a founder and want to own a new business line, not just support one.

Ex-founders and future founders

You've started something before, or plan to, and want an EIR role at a fund or incubator as your next step.

Probably not a fit if you're looking for a job placement guarantee, or for a course on general management. This bootcamp is about building new businesses from zero.

Questions

How much time does it take each week?

About 26–28 hours. Coursework takes 6–8 hours: two to three hours of self-paced lessons, two and a half hours of live sessions, and time to prepare. Project work takes about 20 hours, building the new business you pick in week 2.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted the same day. You need to hand in at least 10 of the 12 weekly assignments to finish the bootcamp.

Do I need my own startup idea?

No. In week 2 you'll pick a new line of business for an existing company, or for your own idea if you have one.

Will EIRcamp get me an EIR job?

We can't promise a job. You'll leave with a portfolio of EIR work, practice at EIR interviews, and a list of companies and funds that hire for these roles.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll launch a first version with no-code tools, or run it by hand at first.
