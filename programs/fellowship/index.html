What's included

Everything below is covered by the one fee. There are no add-ons.

  • 12 Saturday workshops

    90 minutes, live, with exercises in small groups. Recorded.

  • 12 office hours

    Wednesday evenings. Bring a question or a draft.

  • Self-paced lessons

    Two to three hours a week of short videos, readings and templates.

  • Your own mentor

    The same mentor for all 12 weeks, with written feedback on every assignment.

  • A peer pod

    Five founders at your stage who meet weekly and review each other's work.

  • Demo day

    Pitch to mentors and angel investors in week 12.

Who should apply

Working professionals

You have an idea and a job, and want to test the idea properly before you decide to quit.

Students and recent graduates

You want to build something real, with structure and honest feedback from people who've done it.

Early founders without traction

You've started building but don't have paying customers yet, and need a plan for the next three months.

Probably not a fit if you already have meaningful revenue or are raising beyond a seed round. This program is for the stage before your first real customers.

Questions

How much time does it take each week?

Six to eight hours: two to three hours of self-paced lessons, two and a half hours of live sessions, and the rest on your weekly assignment.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted the same day. You need to hand in at least 10 of the 12 weekly assignments to finish the program.

Do I need a registered company?

No. Week 9 covers when and how to register, and whether you need to yet.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll build your first version with no-code tools, or run it by hand at first.

Will I get funding at the end?

We can't promise funding. Fellows who are ready pitch to mentors and angel investors at demo day, and every fellow leaves with a funding plan.
