What's included

Everything below is covered by the one fee. There are no add-ons.

  • 12 Saturday workshops

    90 minutes, live, with exercises in small groups. Recorded.

  • 12 office hours

    Wednesday evenings. Bring a question, a draft or a blocker.

  • Self-paced lessons

    Two to three hours a week of short videos, case studies and templates.

  • 20 hours a week on your startup

    Every assignment is real work on your own startup, not a case study.

  • Your own mentor

    The same mentor for all 12 weeks, with written feedback on every assignment.

  • A founder peer group

    Five founders at a similar stage, who meet weekly and keep each other accountable.

  • Demo day

    Pitch to a panel of founders and investors in week 12.

Week by week

Four stages, three weeks each: validate the idea, build the MVP, grow your first customers, then raise. Open any week to see the live sessions, the self-paced material and what you hand in.

Who should apply

The fellowship is for early-stage founders in India. You don't need a product, a co-founder or funding yet, just an idea you're serious about.

First-time founders

You have an idea, or have just started, and want a clear path from idea to a product people pay for.

Founders who feel stuck

You've been working on it for a while but aren't sure if you're solving the right problem for the right customer.

Founders raising their first cheque

You have early traction and want your numbers, deck and pitch ready before you talk to investors.

Probably not a fit if you're looking for guaranteed funding, or want someone to build the product for you. This fellowship is about you doing the work, with guidance.

Questions

Do I need a startup already?

No. You need an idea you want to work on. If you've already started, you'll use the fellowship to fill the gaps.

How much time does it take each week?

About 26–28 hours. Coursework takes 6–8 hours: two to three hours of self-paced lessons, two and a half hours of live sessions, and time to prepare. The other 20 hours are work on your own startup.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and posted the same day. You need to hand in at least 10 of the 12 weekly assignments to finish the fellowship.

Will I get funding?

We can't promise funding. You'll leave with a tested idea, an MVP, early customers, a financial model and a pitch, and you'll pitch to a panel on demo day.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll build your first version with no-code tools, or run it by hand at first.

Can my co-founder join too?

Yes. Each co-founder applies separately and pays the fee. You'll be placed in the same peer group.
